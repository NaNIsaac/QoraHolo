## [0.1.0] - unreleased

First version, built from source so far. It holds field retrieval from the hologram sideband,
least-squares phase unwrapping, the Rytov approximation, Ewald-sphere mapping, the
Gerchberg-Papoulis and FISTA total-variation missing-cone solvers, and the multi-slice beam
propagation model with its gradient-based inversion, on CPU and CUDA, with Python bindings.
